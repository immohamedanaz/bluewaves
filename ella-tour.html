Ella Sri Lanka Tour

Lush green hills, tea plantations, and the famous Nine Arch Bridge.

Ella is a small town in the Badulla District of Uva Province, situated at an elevation of 1,041 metres above sea level. Famed for its breathtaking views, tea plantations, and the iconic Nine Arches Bridge, Ella is a paradise for nature lovers and hikers.

Tour Itinerary

Discover Sri Lanka's most scenic mountain landscapes

  • Scenic Mountain Drive

    Depart your hotel and enjoy a phenomenal drive up the winding roads through misty tea plantations toward Ella.

  • Hike to Little Adam's Peak

    Take an easy but rewarding hike to the summit of Little Adam's Peak for panoramic views of the cascading mountain ranges.

  • Nine Arch Bridge

    Walk to the iconic Nine Arch Bridge, an architectural marvel set amidst dense jungle and tea fields. We'll wait to catch sight of a passing train.

  • Lunch at Ella Town

    Relax and enjoy a flavorful Sri Lankan lunch at a cozy cafe in the laid-back town of Ella.

  • Ravana Falls Viewpoint

    Stop by the striking Ravana Waterfalls, cascading from an oval-shaped concave rock outcrop, linked to ancient legends.

  • Return Journey

    Begin the comfortable drive back to your hotel as dusk settles over the central highlands.

Ella Day Tour FAQ

Ella is famous for the iconic Nine Arches Bridge, Little Adam's Peak hike, scenic tea plantations, Ravana Falls, and stunning panoramic views of Sri Lanka's hill country. It is a top destination for nature lovers.

Ella is approximately 220 km from Colombo and takes about 5-6 hours by private car. Blue Waves Tours offers comfortable private transfers and day tours from Colombo or your hotel.

The best time to visit Ella is January to April and July to September during dry seasons. The misty hill country atmosphere makes it scenic year-round.
